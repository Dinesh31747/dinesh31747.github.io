Chain Spark Privacy Policy

Draft for developer review. Verify the release checklist before publishing. Remove this notice when publishing the reviewed policy.

Prepared 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Android update with optional cloud saves and rewarded advertising.

Suba Studios develops Chain Spark: Circuit Puzzle. This policy explains how the game uses information for gameplay, optional accounts and cloud saves, analytics, advertising and support.

Your choices

  • You can play without connecting a Unity Player Account. Guest progress is saved on your device.
  • Connecting an account enables cloud backup and recovery of that account's progress.
  • You can change Share playtest data in Shop & Settings. In this version, this setting is on by default on a fresh installation. Turning it off stops the game's future Firebase analytics collection on that installation; it does not delete records already submitted or control advertising providers.
  • Rewarded ads are offered for optional in-game benefits. Advertising services may process information when they initialize or request an ad, before a video is watched.

Progress on your device and in the cloud

The game stores unlocked levels, stars, Sparks, hints, reward and daily-puzzle history, selected theme, tutorial progress and preferences locally. Guest saves and connected-account saves are kept separate. Signing out opens the separate guest save. Unsynced local account progress is retained for that same account so it can be recovered when the account reconnects; signing out does not delete the account.

If you connect an account, Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save process a player identifier and service information such as IP addresses to authenticate you and store game progress. The uploaded game save includes levels, stars, Sparks, hints, theme selection and reward history. Cloud backup and recovery require a successful connection. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local progress that has not been uploaded.

Unity Player Accounts

Sign-in opens Unity's account service in your browser. Unity handles account details, including the email address used for account creation and a Unity Player Account identifier. The game uses authentication tokens and player identifiers; Suba Studios does not ask you to send your account password. Unity's separate player account can be used with other supported games and is governed by Unity's privacy policy.

Playtest and gameplay analytics

When Share playtest data is enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives gameplay events such as level starts, wins, failures, retries, hints and tap rescues. Events may include level and attempt numbers, campaign or daily mode, elapsed time, taps used or remaining, and Sparks spent. Firebase also processes app-instance identifiers, app and operating-system information, session activity and approximate location derived from IP addresses.

The Android build is configured to disable Firebase Analytics' collection of Android advertising IDs. This setting does not disable advertising-ID use by separate advertising SDKs. See Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

Chain Spark uses Unity LevelPlay mediation. Advertising services used for this release include ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob. Depending on the provider, device settings and applicable privacy choices, advertising services process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, ad requests, views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These services use information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain their services. Ad-related information may be shared with advertising partners.

For provider privacy information and controls, see Unity / ironSource and Google. You can also use the advertising-ID controls available in your Android settings. Turning off gameplay analytics does not turn off advertising data processing.

Support information

If you email hello.subastudios@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request, investigate a problem or verify a data request. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or payment-card information.

Retention and security

Local saves remain until removed through app controls, clearing app storage or uninstalling, subject to the device's own backup behaviour. Cloud progress is retained until the game account or its saved data is deleted. Signing out alone does not request deletion.

Our published Google Analytics retention settings are two months for event data and fourteen months for user data, with new activity restarting the user-data retention period. Google applies its scheduled deletion processes, and aggregated reporting can remain for longer. Advertising and Unity account services have their own retention practices described in their linked policies. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations.

Authentication, cloud-save and the named analytics and advertising services use encrypted connections for data sent over the network. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security. These providers may process information outside your country.

Delete your game account or request data deletion

You can request deletion without having the app installed. Use our Chain Spark account and data deletion page, or email hello.subastudios@gmail.com with the subject Chain Spark account deletion.

In the app, open Save Progress / Cloud Save, then Account Options, then Delete Game Cloud Account. The game asks for confirmation. This removes the Chain Spark cloud backups and game cloud account and removes that account's local archive on the device performing deletion. The separate guest save remains. Deletion cannot remotely erase offline files on other devices; contact us if you need help removing remaining copies.

Deleting your Chain Spark game account does not delete the separate Unity Player Account, and deleting the Unity Player Account alone does not necessarily delete your Chain Spark data. The deletion page explains both routes. Requests about analytics, advertising or support correspondence are handled according to the information we can identify and the relevant provider's processes. Where records must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons, we will explain the applicable exception.

Age and policy changes

Chain Spark is intended for players aged 13 and older. Account and advertising services may impose additional age requirements depending on your location. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information inappropriately. We update this page when the game's data practices change.

SUBA STUDIOS · CHAIN SPARK: CIRCUIT PUZZLE

Chain Spark Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Suba Studios, operated by Dinesh Jataprolu in India, develops Chain Spark: Circuit Puzzle. This policy explains how information is used for gameplay, optional accounts and cloud saves, cosmetics, purchases where enabled, rewarded advertising, analytics and support.

Your main choices
  • You can play as a guest without connecting a Unity Player Account.
  • Account connection enables cloud backup and recovery. Paid cosmetics, where available, require account connection so ownership can be checked.
  • You can turn off Share playtest data in Shop & Settings. This controls the game’s future Firebase analytics collection on that installation; it does not delete existing records or control advertising providers.
  • You can request account deletion or deletion of specific data while keeping your account.

1. Game progress and cosmetic settings

The game stores levels, stars, Sparks, hints, rewards, daily-puzzle history, tutorial progress and preferences on your device. Cosmetic information can include Sparks unlocks, selected theme, trail, arrows and receivers, background and sound, and the expiry time of a rewarded-ad trial.

Guest saves and connected-account saves are kept separate. Signing out returns to the separate guest save. Unsynced progress for a connected account is retained locally for that same account; signing out is not deletion. Cosmetic trial timers and equipment preferences are stored locally for the relevant profile and are not guaranteed to transfer to another device.

If you connect an account, Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save process a player identifier and service information, such as IP addresses, to authenticate you and store game progress. Uploaded saves include gameplay progress, Sparks, hints, reward history, supported theme selections and Sparks cosmetic unlocks. Paid purchase ownership is checked separately from game-save backups.

Cloud backup requires a successful connection. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local progress that has not been uploaded.

2. Unity Player Accounts

Sign-in opens Unity’s account service in your browser. Unity handles account details, including the email address used to create the account and a Unity Player Account identifier. The game uses authentication tokens and player identifiers; Suba Studios does not ask you to send your password. Unity’s separate player account may be used with other supported games and is covered by Unity’s privacy policy.

3. Google Play purchases and verification

This section applies when you purchase or restore paid cosmetics in a version where in-app purchases are enabled. Google Play handles checkout and payment details. Suba Studios does not receive your full payment-card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking password.

To check a purchase and associate it with the correct game account, the app sends the cosmetic product ID and Google Play purchase token to our verification service with a Unity authentication token. The service checks the authenticated player identifier and uses Google Play’s purchase API to obtain purchase status and account-association information. Google Play also receives a hashed game-player identifier when checkout is started. Hashing makes this identifier pseudonymous; it does not make it anonymous.

This information is used to deliver and restore purchased cosmetics, prevent invalid or duplicate grants, keep different players’ purchases separate, and check cancelled, refunded or revoked purchases. Google Cloud hosts the verification service when it is enabled. The verifier processes purchase checks without maintaining a separate purchase database. Google Play retains its transaction records under its own policies. Hosting services can process connection metadata such as IP address, request time, response status and user-agent information for service operation and security.

If you contact support about a purchase, we may ask for the Google Play order ID, product name and relevant account details. Do not email raw purchase tokens or authentication tokens. See Google’s privacy policy and our Terms of Service.

4. Gameplay analytics

Share playtest data is on by default on a fresh installation of the described version. When enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives gameplay events such as level starts, wins, failures, retries, hints and tap rescues. Events may include level and attempt numbers, campaign or daily mode, elapsed time, taps used or remaining, and Sparks spent.

Firebase also processes app-instance identifiers, app and operating-system information, session activity and approximate location derived from IP addresses. The Android configuration disables Firebase Analytics’ collection of Android advertising IDs. This does not disable advertising-ID processing by separate advertising SDKs. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

5. Rewarded advertising and trials

Chain Spark uses Unity LevelPlay mediation. Advertising services used for this release include ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. Depending on the provider, device settings and privacy choices, these services process device or advertising identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, ad requests, views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

Advertising providers use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain their services. Ad-related information may be shared with advertising partners. Processing can begin when an advertising service initializes or requests an ad, before a video is watched. Choosing not to watch an ad does not necessarily stop all SDK processing.

Rewarded ads offer optional benefits such as hints or time-limited cosmetic access. The game records reward delivery and, for cosmetic trials, an expiry time. A trial does not itself create a paid purchase. See Unity / ironSource privacy information. Android advertising-ID controls and any privacy controls presented by the advertising service may provide additional choices. Turning off gameplay analytics does not turn off advertising processing.

6. Support and website access

If you email hello.subastudios@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond, investigate problems and verify data requests. Avoid sending passwords, one-time codes or payment credentials.

These policy pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information when you visit them, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. These pages contain no added analytics scripts, advertising scripts or tracking cookies.

7. Retention

How different records are retained
Information Retention and deletion
Local game saves and cosmetic settings Remain until removed through relevant game controls, clearing app storage or uninstalling, subject to Android backup behaviour. Expired trial access stops; its stored expiry record is removed when the game checks it.
Connected game account and cloud progress Retained until the game account or the relevant saved data is deleted. Signing out alone does not delete them.
Firebase analytics Our published settings are two months for event data and fourteen months for user data, with new activity restarting the user-data retention period. Google applies scheduled deletion; aggregated reporting can remain longer.
Purchase verification The verifier uses submitted tokens to process the request and does not keep a separate purchase database. Google Play maintains its transaction history. Infrastructure and security logs follow the hosting service’s configured retention and applicable audit requirements; contact us for the retention applicable to a specific record.
Support and deletion correspondence Kept as needed to resolve requests, document their handling and meet applicable legal or security obligations. If a retention exception applies to your request, we will explain the data, reason and applicable period.
Advertising and provider accounts Subject to the respective providers’ policies and deletion processes. We will assist with requests concerning identifiable data processed for the game.

8. Security, service providers and international processing

Authentication, cloud saving and purchase verification use encrypted network connections, as do the named analytics and advertising services. No storage or transmission system guarantees absolute security. Service providers may process information outside your country. Their roles include authentication and cloud storage (Unity), analytics and payments (Google), verification hosting (Google Cloud where enabled), advertising (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads), and hosting these pages (GitHub).

9. Delete your account or specific data

Visit our account and data deletion page. It explains how to delete the Chain Spark game account and its associated data, or request deletion of specific data while keeping the account. No app installation or website login is needed to submit an email request. We may need proportionate verification to protect your information.

In the app, open Save Progress / Cloud Save → Account Options → Delete Game Cloud Account and confirm. The game removes its cloud-save records and game cloud account and clears the corresponding local progress archive on the device completing the operation. The separate guest save remains. Additional local preferences or offline copies on other devices may require separate cleanup; an account deletion request covers associated data within our control, not just cloud progress.

Deleting a Chain Spark game account does not automatically delete the separate Unity Player Account or Google Play transaction history, and does not automatically issue a refund. Contact us for purchase-access assistance. We will handle associated data held by our service providers through their applicable processes and explain any justified retention exceptions.

10. Age, changes and contact

Chain Spark is intended for players aged 13 and older. Account and advertising services may impose additional age requirements depending on location. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information inappropriately. We update this page when the game’s data practices change.

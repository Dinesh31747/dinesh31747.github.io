Chain Spark Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Suba Studios develops Chain Spark. This policy covers the Android playtest with advertising turned off.

Your choice

You can play with Share playtest data off. This is the default on a fresh installation. If you turn it on in the main menu, Suba Studios uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn where puzzles are too hard, too easy, or unclear. You can turn it off in the same place at any time. Turning it off stops future playtest analytics collection on that installation. Previously submitted records follow the retention settings below.

What the game saves on your device

The game stores progress, unlocked levels, Sparks, hints, game preferences and your sharing choice locally on your device. You can remove these local records by clearing Chain Spark’s app storage in Android settings. Clearing storage also resets your game progress.

What the playtest sends when you enable sharing

The game records level starts, wins, out-of-taps failures, retries, hints used and tap rescues. An event can include the level and attempt numbers, campaign or daily mode, elapsed time, taps spent and remaining, the source of a rescue and any Sparks spent on it.

Google Analytics uses an app-instance ID to distinguish installations. Analytics also processes information such as app version, device and operating system, session activity and approximate location derived from the device’s IP address. The playtest is configured to disable Android advertising ID collection by Firebase Analytics.

Google processes analytics data for Suba Studios through Firebase. The SDK transmits analytics data using encrypted HTTPS connections. Firebase privacy and security information.

How long Google Analytics keeps data

For this playtest, we set event-data retention to 2 months and user-data retention to 14 months. New activity restarts the 14-month retention period for the user identifier. The event retention period runs separately. Google processes expired records for deletion on its regular schedule. Standard reports based on aggregated statistics can remain available for longer. Google Analytics retention details.

Support and deletion requests

For a privacy question or to request access to or deletion of playtest data, email Suba Studios at suadstudios@gmail.com. Mention Chain Spark and the approximate date you played. Gameplay events are tied to an installation identifier, so we may need more information to locate the relevant records. We will explain what we can identify and delete.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer and follow up on your request.

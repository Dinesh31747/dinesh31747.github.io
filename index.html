04 / MOBILE MERGE In development

MergeForge

A mobile merge game with connected progression systems.

Generate and merge alchemical items, fulfil customer orders and manage space on a 56-cell board.

  • Generators, merging and progressive customer orders
  • Save/load, inventory and timed trapped bubbles
  • Daily login, double-coin bonuses and rewarded-ad flows
  • Mobile UI, sound and visual feedback
Save/loadInventoryProgressive ordersRewardsMobile UI
Full gameplay recording ↗
Project details & credits +

Production & merging: Five generator tiers, 12 normal merge levels, five rare levels, energy, cooldowns and upgrades.

Save/load & inventory: Persisted board state, stash slots, orders, currencies, progression and timers; permanent and temporary inventory unlocks.

Orders & progression: Single- and multi-item orders, matching feedback, XP and levels. New ordinary Potion orders use production effort and the generators currently on the board.

Daily & idle rewards: Daily login/streak rewards, offline rewards and double-coin reward flows.

Trapped bubbles: Timed bonus items with claim/expiry behaviour and safeguards against using unclaimed items.

UI, sound & effects: Portrait HUD, item and upgrade panels, order animations, audio events, merge particles and drag feedback.

Ad integration status: Eight game-side rewarded placements cover cooldown skips, order reward doubling, bubble claims, idle coin doubling, energy refills, order refreshes, stash unlocks and streak doubling. Daily caps and mock/test paths are implemented; live ad SDK validation is pending.

Orders based on production effort: Integrated and validated an expected-taps evaluator across five generator tiers and 12 target levels. Quick, Standard and Grand profiles guide new ordinary orders; held requirements and rewards retain stable identities across reloads. Special multi-item orders use a separate existing generation path.

Reliability & testing: Worked on stateful object pooling, slot reservations, safe save restoration and stale-callback guards. A rapid-drag bug was traced to Rigidbody interpolation restoring an old pose; the fix was checked on PC, a physical Android APK and Unity Remote.

My contribution & credits: Solo game direction, Unity integration, prefab and ScriptableObject configuration, interface assembly, testing and iteration. Visuals combine AI-generated artwork with basic sprites and my own setup.

Development status: In development; also known internally as Merge Shop. The video shows an earlier build and may not show all current features.

HOW I WORK

The detail is
in the interaction.

A mechanic can work and still feel unfinished. I like going back to those moments.

01

Build something playable

Start with simple geometry and a focused interaction. Put the idea in Unity and see how it plays.

02

Pay attention to the response

A mirror turning, a spell firing, an item landing. Look at the movement and feedback from the player’s side.

03

Test, adjust, play again

Work through small changes, check them in the game, and keep refining what the player sees and feels.

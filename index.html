HOW I WORK

The detail is
in the interaction.

A mechanic can work and still feel unfinished. I like going back to those moments.

01

Build something playable

Start with simple geometry and a focused interaction. Put the idea in Unity and see how it plays.

02

Pay attention to the response

A mirror turning, a spell firing, an item landing. Look at the movement and feedback from the player’s side.

03

Test, adjust, play again

Work through small changes, check them in the game, and keep refining what the player sees and feels.

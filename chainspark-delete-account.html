Delete your Chain Spark account or data

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Use this page to request deletion of your Chain Spark: Circuit Puzzle game account or associated data from Suba Studios. You do not need to reinstall the game or sign in to this website to submit a request.

Delete my game account

Request removal of your Chain Spark game account and associated data, including cloud progress.

Request account deletion

Delete data, keep my account

Request deletion of specified data, such as cloud progress, identifiable analytics records or support correspondence, without deleting your game account.

Request data-only deletion

If an email button does not open your mail app, write to hello.subastudios@gmail.com. Use the subject Chain Spark account deletion or Chain Spark data deletion - keep account.

What to include

  1. Tell us whether to delete the game account or keep it and delete only specified data.
  2. Contact us using the email associated with your Unity Player Account if possible. Include a game Player ID or Unity Player Account ID only if you already have it. Do not reinstall solely to obtain an identifier.
  3. Describe the data you want deleted. For purchase-support records, an order ID may help locate them; do not send raw purchase or authentication tokens.
Protect your account details.

Never send passwords, one-time verification codes, full card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or bank credentials. We may ask for limited additional information to verify ownership and locate the right records.

What happens after you request deletion

  1. We review the request and explain any necessary verification.
  2. We clarify the affected data and any consequences, such as lost progress or effects on restoring purchased cosmetics. A data-only request does not authorize deletion of your whole account.
  3. We delete the requested identifiable data within our control and use relevant service-provider processes for associated data they process for the game.
  4. We confirm completion or explain what remains, why it must be retained and the applicable retention period.

Sending an email starts a request; it does not instantly delete data. We will explain the expected timeframe and act as soon as reasonably possible, within applicable legal deadlines. Where data cannot be linked to you or an exception applies, we will explain this. We do not require you to make a purchase to request deletion.

Delete your game account inside the app

  1. Open Chain Spark and connect the game account you want to delete.
  2. Open Save Progress / Cloud Save.
  3. Select Account Options → Delete Game Cloud Account.
  4. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion. A working connection is required.

If the operation fails or requests support, email us using the account-deletion option above. Do not repeatedly clear app storage to try to finish a server-side deletion. Signing out is not account deletion.

What is deleted and what may remain

Data or service Effect of deletion
Chain Spark game account and cloud saves Account deletion removes the game’s Unity Authentication player account and associated cloud-save records, including uploaded levels, stars, Sparks, hints, reward history and Sparks cosmetic unlocks. A data-only request removes the agreed records while retaining the account.
Device files In-app deletion clears the corresponding local progress archive on the device completing the operation. Other local cosmetic preferences, trial records or offline device copies may remain until separately removed. Clear Chain Spark’s app storage to erase all local profiles and preferences on that device; this also removes unsynced progress.
Guest progress The separate guest save is not removed by deleting a connected account. Specify it if relevant to your request. Clearing app storage removes it on that device.
Paid cosmetics and purchase checks Account deletion can affect restoration because purchases are associated with a game-player identifier. It does not automatically refund a purchase or transfer it to another account. The verifier does not maintain a separate purchase database; request deletion of identifiable support or operational records through us.
Google Play transaction history Deleting the game account does not delete Google’s transaction records. Google may retain payment and transaction information under its own policies and legal obligations. For refunds, use Google Play’s refund process or contact us about a purchase problem.
Analytics, ads and support records These are not all removed by the game’s cloud-delete button. Our account-deletion process also reviews associated identifiable data and relevant provider requests. You may request these data separately while keeping your account. Aggregated or de-identified information may no longer be attributable to you.
Retention exceptions Limited records may need to remain for legitimate legal, transaction, fraud-prevention or security purposes. We will identify any such records and explain the reason and applicable period. The Privacy Policy describes retention by data category.

Keep deleted progress from uploading again

If you request cloud-progress deletion while keeping the account, tell us if you play on multiple devices. Existing offline saves can otherwise be uploaded later. We will explain the relevant sign-out, sync or local-cleanup steps before carrying out the requested change. Do not delete unrelated saves or your whole account unless that is what you intend.

Your separate Unity Player Account

Chain Spark’s game account and your Unity Player Account are separate. To manage or delete the latter, use Unity Player Accounts and its account or support controls. Deleting either one does not necessarily delete all records held by the other. Requests about Unity’s player account must follow Unity’s verification process.

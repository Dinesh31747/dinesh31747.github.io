Delete your Chain Spark account or data

Use this page to request deletion of your Chain Spark: Circuit Puzzle game account or associated data from Suba Studios. You do not need to reinstall the game to send a request.

Request deletion by email

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, send an email manually to hello.subastudios@gmail.com with the subject Chain Spark account deletion.

  1. Say whether you want to delete your Chain Spark game account and cloud progress, or request deletion of analytics/support data.
  2. For an account request, contact us using the email associated with your Unity Player Account if possible. Include your game Player ID or Unity Player Account ID if you already have it. Do not reinstall the game solely to obtain an identifier.
  3. We may request additional information to locate the right records and verify that the account is yours. Never send a password or a one-time verification code.

We will explain any verification needed, the expected completion timeframe and any data that cannot be identified or must be retained. Requests are handled as soon as reasonably possible and within applicable legal deadlines. Sending an email starts a request; it does not instantly delete your account.

Delete through the game

  1. Open Chain Spark and sign into the game account you want to delete.
  2. Open Save Progress / Cloud Save from the main menu.
  3. Select Account Options, then Delete Game Cloud Account.
  4. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

What is deleted and what remains

  • Game account deletion: removes the Chain Spark Unity Authentication player account and its cloud-save records, including uploaded levels, stars, Sparks, hints and reward history.
  • Local files: in-app deletion also removes that account's local archive on the device performing deletion. A website/email request cannot remotely clear an offline device. Clear Chain Spark's app storage to remove all locally stored game profiles on that device; this also removes any unsynced progress.
  • Guest progress: the separate guest save is not deleted by deleting a connected game account.
  • Analytics and ads: deleting game progress does not automatically delete separate provider records. Mention these in your request; we will explain the available identification and deletion routes. Aggregated or de-identified records may no longer be attributable to an individual.
  • Retention exceptions: any records retained for legal or security reasons will be identified in our response, with the reason and applicable retention period.

Your separate Unity Player Account

To manage or delete your Unity Player Account, visit Unity Player Accounts and use its account/support controls. This is separate from deleting the Chain Spark game account. Unity requires requests about its player account to come directly from the player.
